## The lead's reading

The bearings: `take a bearing of manacle` and `of manacles` were refused while "manacle Point" was in the list, since the name must match a feature's words whole (and the Manacles proper, the danger, were not in sight: the watcher's note that they were never raised passing two or three miles off); a bearing should take a feature by any of its words when one in sight matches, and say so when two do. `belay heave the lead` and `avast heave lead` were refused because the work's log name is "heaving lead" and the matcher wanted it word for word; package 29c's rule, "by the order's words", should take `heave the lead`. `tack ship` was refused from a reach: a tack from a reach should bring her by the wind first (the follow-ups). `Set the mainsail, one reef` is a natural compound the grammar could take as set then reef. `Reef the topsail, one reef` on a topsail with one band says "(1 reefs in)", a plural to fix. The two sheet eases were right: the headsail sheets were already at their geometry's end.
