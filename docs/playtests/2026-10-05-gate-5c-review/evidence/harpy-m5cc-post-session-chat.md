# The brig's officer after the m5c-c run: its own summary

Supplied by the owner on 2026-10-06, from the chat of game 9 (the merchant brig the officer
calls the *Harpy*, Falmouth to Roscoff to Brest, on build m5c-c/37d; Opus 5.5 as officer of
the watch from Roscoff). This is the chat side of the session, which no save holds. It is the
model's own account and opinion, kept as the playtest form's section 9 asks; the review's
report (section 10) says which parts the log, a replay and the code bear out, and which turn
out to be something other than they looked from the deck.

## The officer

> The Harpy is moored in Brest road in 14 fathoms on mud, both bowers down, with the Brest
> pilot aboard. I've handed the deck back and the game is saved. Fog came down about two hours
> after we anchored, so we got in just in time. The purser's price list has brandy at £110
> against the £100 you paid at Roscoff.
>
> Worth fixing first:
>
> - Ground tackle.
>   - A veer goes past the number you give it: "veer to 75" ran out to 228 fathoms, "180" to
>     240 and "45" to 67.
>   - Anchors report "dragging" while the cable shows almost no strain.
>   - With two anchors down, the slack one keeps setting off the drag alarm even though the
>     ship isn't moving.
>   - "Weigh the best bower" or "weigh the small bower" brings up whichever anchor she's riding
>     by, not the one named.
> - Heaving to. She spins out of it twice: no sternway builds up, so the helm has nothing to
>   work with. "Fill away" then picks the old course or the wrong tack, and every square sail
>   comes aback.
> - Reckoning.
>   - A lunar trusted only to 25 miles replaced an account good to a mile. You said this was
>     meant to be fixed in this version.
>   - The noon latitude (48°07′ by sight against 48°13′ by account) wasn't applied to the
>     account.
>   - A bearing that left the position unchanged still cut the doubt from 3 miles to 1.
>   - The doubt also got smaller while she drifted becalmed on the tide.
> - Shaping a course. It now warns about named rocks along the line, which helped a lot. But
>   it doesn't check the coastline, and a waypoint I put off Petit Minou turned out to be on
>   land. Your "show me the chart" idea would solve this.
>
> Smaller items:
>
> - "Trim sails" refuses with "no wind to trim to" in a knot or two of air.
> - "Heave short" won't take a number of fathoms.
> - The log still says "by the captain's order" for all hands I called under your grant.
> - The readings sometimes list me as "below, asleep" while I hold the deck.
> - Waiting for "six bells" in the last dog watch never wakes me, since that watch doesn't
>   strike six.
> - There's no fog bell, and "the chart ends here" reads as if the world stops.
> - I can't see which orders are queued waiting for hands.
>
> Working well: the cross-bearing fixes (the three-mark ones especially), the line-of-danger
> warnings, the deep-sea lead and its ground descriptions, the pilot's hail and directions, and
> sending the boat ashore for prices.
>
> My own mistakes this passage were turning toward the wind before taking in the stuns'ls,
> letting out far too much cable in a tight berth in the Goulet, and misreading the sheets
> twice. All three are written up in my in-game journal, along with the full bug list.
