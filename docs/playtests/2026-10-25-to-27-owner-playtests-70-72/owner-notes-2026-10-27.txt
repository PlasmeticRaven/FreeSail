11. Major design consideration: the world must be coherent between any arbitrary combination of player and model in various stations, across ships, as people, and regardless of distance to the player. If I have a model captain of an armed tender of my player ship, they must be able to function in the world as well as if they were captain of the player ship (going ashore, trading, interacting, sailing anywhere else arbitrarily, combat). This also extends to any stationed person, a person should be able to be in a port somewhere totally different from the player and still interact with the world, extending to getting on a ship and eventually ending up where the player is. We'll possibly need to reconsider, or strongly reorient the current implementation of the "world" so that it generalizes beyond the player. In essence, we don't want a model on another ship to be existing in a "basic" version of the world or station to the extent that they can reasonably interact with the world as a player might (we don't need full detail ships everywhere, but we might consider a ship with a stationed person on it at full detail at all times, or at least allowing any ship to be able to interact with the systems of the world needed for important interactions and information, but not every costly part of a full detail ship).
12. National/company leaders as people that can be stationed, for control of the naval and economic systems.
