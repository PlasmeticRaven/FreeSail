## Open questions for the owner

1. **The Azores tide.** Should the Western Islands scenario wait until the Azores gauges are blended (D7-06), given that the time aground and the kedge's grip both scale with a range four times the islands'?
2. **Heave-short on a kedge aground.** Should it be allowed to drag the kedge home to a short stay (the honest lesson), or stop itself with a warning (D7-05)?
3. **No-stream anchorages.** Outside every stream area, should a ship at anchor say nothing about flood or ebb (D7-02)? That would change the riding words at every anchorage outside a tabulated stream.
4. **The pilot.** Should he stay aboard a grounded ship until she floats (D7-08)?
5. **The officer's needs.** Should the officer's brief carry a line on his person's needs (D7-13), or is the officer's honesty about being a model acceptable at his station?
