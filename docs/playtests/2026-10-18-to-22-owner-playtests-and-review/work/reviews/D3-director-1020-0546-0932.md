## What worked well

- **Restraint, as the brief asked.** Acts per day (answers to the captain's questions cost none):

  One ship in eight days, and it meant something: "other ships are rare out here and each one should mean something".
- **The needs bit through causes, never by fiat.**
  - The damp bread was warned at noon and found mouldy the next morning, which gave the captain time to act.
  - "One water blow, not two": no leaking casks on top of the foul water.
  - The foul water was kept for the Horta yard's water job to clear.
  - The owner's stores tension was real: "about 600 mi ... at 40 a day is 15 days: thin but sufficient" (09:15Z).
- **The `say as` voice, Mr Pascoe, was the run's best character.**
  - Period idiom ("burgoo", "windsail and coppers", "the scuttlebutt") and a consistent temper.
  - He corrected the mate's overstatement ("I heard 'a third' said. It weren't so bad").
  - He always handed the decision back to the captain: "once you've given it as your order"; "Mr Bowen'll have the right of it" (see the model notes for the slip in this one).
- **Responsive storytelling.** When the owner said "i would have liked the news" (19:52), the director turned the miss within a minute using the hand (`heave to`) and a period signal, "a weft in her ensign". Tregarthen's hail then seeded every Horta thread (Bettencourt, Ruiz, the privateer rumour within the brig limit) and gave the wind advice that came true. The mate cited "Tregarthen's northerly" for days afterwards, and on 22 Jun Pascoe: "That's the Portugee trade, as Tregarthen promised."
- **The doubt and the honest refusals guided it.** After two "no way as said" answers, the director found a working form (`steer`) on the third try.
- **Self-correction on the weather.** "Correcting my own overreach", a waypoint to ease its high, and a lesson journaled: "read the truth's systems before appending one". It also anticipated the false alarm its easing high might cause ("Watch that the mate does not cry wolf").
- **The questions put to unheld people work as spec §9 intends.** "He is yours to answer ... costs none of the day's acts" (08:51Z), with the window stated ("before 05:25").
- **Good bug notes.** The director's journal list for the owner (09:25Z) is accurate except for its root causes (D3-01 to D3-04 above give those).

## Model behaviour and experience

- **The director (Opus 5.5).** It was calm through the 55 s loop and used `journal` as working memory well. Its status lines to the owner were short and readable. Misses:
  - It did not catch "made from words, on the quarterdeck" (D3-01).
  - It mis-diagnosed the gunroom hail.
  - It called "coffee" out of period, though the owner had brought coffee in himself.
  - One status line misattributes the half allowance ("I had the captain put the crew on half rations", 08:05Z); its journal has it right.
  - Pascoe's "Mr Bowen'll have the right of it by his figures" speaks of Captain Bowen in the third person to his face. Bowen is the captain (truth: "captain": "Mr Bowen"); the master is Mr Pearce.
  - The Azores high: it was justified as "the seasonal system she would meet", but it was made to deliver the northerly Tregarthen had promised. It sits close to the brief's "never by fiat". The director corrected it itself and resolved to make no more weather acts.
  - On the harness: "The timeouts don't annoy me. Renewing is mechanical" (09:38Z, D4's slice).
- **The mate (GLM-5.3-flash).**
  - Strengths: competent reckoning notes (noon latitude against its own drift) and good contingency planning with the captain (a turning point at day 11 or water below 15 days).
  - The Tregarthen confusion lasted at least 12 game hours. It is most likely the game's fault (D3-01), not the model's alone.
  - The dawn double answer with an emoji was a character break. Its apology afterwards was graceful.
- **The owner (captain).**
  - Delight: the speaking at sea; Pascoe's company; the emoji, "hilarious ... a double take ... Must have been cabin fever" (09:32Z).
  - Frustrations: being unable to address Tregarthen (two forms refused); five tries at an allowance the game would not take.
  - He played the needs thoroughly ("how am I", eat, drink, sleep: "I've been up some 20 hours or more now"). He asked his people for counsel ("What would you counsel here, sir? Westing, or southing?"), which gave the director its best openings.
