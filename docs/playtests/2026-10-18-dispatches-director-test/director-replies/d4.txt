This packet goes to Plymouth, into the hand of Mr Veale, who will be on the Barbican by the fish quay from this evening and every evening until it comes; into his hand and no other's, and you will not show it to any officer of the King's, nor say you carry it. Good day to you, sir, and a quick passage.\""
