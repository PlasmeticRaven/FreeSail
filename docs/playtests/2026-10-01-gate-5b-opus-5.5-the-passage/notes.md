# Playtest 12: gate 5b, the frigate's passage, Ushant to Falmouth (Opus 5.5 through Claude Desktop)

The owner's run of gate 5b's item 1 (the frigate's passage, `data/scenarios/gate-5b-passage.yaml`, seed 7, the gate-m5b build) in the browser, with Opus 5.5 at the watcher's station through Claude Desktop (the consent record of 2026-09-29). The owner's notes are below as written; the watcher's post-session note is `post-session-notes.md` and its journal `journal.md`, both verbatim; the save is `save.json` with its checkpoint `save.checkpoint` (tick 53749, 18:55 on 10 June 1805). ## 1. The scenario, the seed and the build

The gate's passage, seed 7, the `gate-m5b` release; the browser; the watcher through Claude Desktop; played from 04:00 to 18:55 ship's time. The owner gave fifty-five orders at the prompt beside the scenario's book, several of them on the watcher's advice and several to find the grammar's edges (section 4).

## 2. What happened, in the owner's words (and the watcher's)

The passage ran as the book intended: the noon sight at 11:59, the ship brought to and the deep-sea lead (fifty-three fathoms, fine grey sand with black specks), the course shaped afresh, the Lizard raised at 15:42, the lead going in, and the ship wearing at the outer road and lying to on the starboard tack in seventeen fathoms at 18:50, about 99 miles in fourteen hours fifty, no damage. Where it needed a captain: the course shaped for Falmouth ran straight for the Manacles (dead ahead at two miles at 17:32) and the owner hauled off to NE by N on the watcher's word; "keep her full" bore her away toward the Old Wall at 18:35; the heave-to at noon did not hold through the cast (she came up, every sail went aback for four minutes, then paid off and gathered four knots before the lead went); and nothing set again the sail taken in to heave to once she filled away.
