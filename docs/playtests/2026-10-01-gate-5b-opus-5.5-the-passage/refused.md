## The lead's reading

Seven of the fifteen are the owner typing a reading's name at the prompt (`the reckoning`, `the master`, `the bearing of the Lizard`, `what is the bearing of the lizard`, `ask the master the reckoning`, the two `reckoning's uncertainty` forms), which the gate report's item 2 told them to do and which the grammar does not take: a reading is a row of the registry that the instruments show, a rule tests and a model asks for, but the prompt has no form for asking it. That is the lead's error in the report and a gap worth closing: a reading's words typed at the prompt should be answered in the log as a query, as `the booms` and `the sail room` are. Three are aliases to add (`steer for Falmouth`, `take a sounding`, `trim`/`tend the spanker sheet`); `work up a reckoning` wants the article's variant; `take a bearing of the Lizard` at 12:37 was right to be refused (the Lizard was not in sight for three hours more). The starter book's `sound the well` is refused at every scenario's start because the well is not yet a reading (spec M4 §24 item 4): the line is noise in every log since milestone 4a and the rule should be held, not refused, until the well exists.
