## Your scope: four parts, and it does not grow

1. **A game can be ended from the harbour.** The owner: "an option to end the game from the harbor would be good, so that a running game can be ended without restarting the harbor or starting a new game." Today `freesail/ui/harbour.py` can start a game (`Harbour.start`, `/api/harbour/start`) and has no way to end one. Ending a game: the clock stopped and the world let go, any door of a model's station closed as the harbour closes one (`stop_door`), the page back at the harbour with no game running and able to begin or take up another; nothing saved is touched, and what was not saved is lost only after the player is told so and says yes. Mind the lock's order (the harbour's first, then the driver's; see the comment in `Harbour.start` and `tests/test_harbour.py::test_a_page_that_asks_while_a_game_starts_finds_one_whole_game`): a page that asks while a game ends must find one whole game or none. With it, since it is the same table: **the stations' table keeps one game's choice of doors into the next** (`client/harbour.js`); a game begun after another offers the stations as the new scenario gives them.
2. **Fog comes on and clears over a time, and is said as it does.** The owner: "fog clears instantaneously. It might be better for it to clear over a period of time, depending on the degree of additional weather simulation necessary. Perhaps, at least, 'the fog seems to be clearing', or, 'a fog is settling in', or the like." Today `freesail/world/weather.py` (`coastal_fog`, about line 1623; the lines "Fog came down." and "The fog lifted.", about lines 249 to 333 and 1505 to 1536) goes between fog with a cable's sight and clear at one moment. Wanted: the sight closing and opening through the steps the weather already has between clear and a cable, over a time with its ground said (a source if you find one, else JUDGEMENT), a line as it begins to thicken and as it begins to thin, deterministic and the same after a save. Everything that reads the sight (the lookout, the far ships' lines, the signals, a model's readings, the director's weather order and its brief) is listed and shown to follow. **Stop and report before building if a recorded passage or more than ten pinned results would move.**
3. **The game's own captain replaces a spar carried away.** The owner, of the privateer Renard in *the first action*: "I noticed she did not attempt to replace the topgallant mast or yard." In his save her main topgallant mast was shot away; in the three hours after, her people plugged her shot holes and set up her rigging, and the spar was never shifted. The game has the work for a player (`data/evolutions/shift_spar.yaml`, `fish_spar.yaml`, `jury_mast.yaml`, `sway_up_topgallant_masts.yaml`), and the captain orders a sprung spar fished (`freesail/world/captains.py`, about line 4741). Wanted: out of action and with hands to spare, he orders a spar carried away replaced where she carries the spare for it, in the order a seaman would (what keeps her manageable first), says so in his intent, and leaves it where she has none; never under fire. Say first what spares a ship carries today and where that is written.
4. **`lie a-try` ordered by the game's own captain with the wind abeam is not taken** (found measuring the *Diana*: ordered at 3,240 s, she sailed on at 10.7 knots; the frigate lay a-try). Reproduce it on a committed ship first; mend it if it is the captain's or the order's; report it and stop if you cannot reproduce it on a ship in the game.

**Not in scope:** the first passage's lesson (the owner placed it in a later milestone); how ships go about or shorten sail (package 125); what the log says of waits and of filling away (package 127); the weather's systems beyond fog's coming and going.
