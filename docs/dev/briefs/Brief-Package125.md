## What was found (measured by another builder; verify it, do not take it on trust)

A new ship not yet on the trunk, the corvette *Diana* (a light ship, heavily canvassed for her weight, with a small rudder), will not tack in a fresh breeze under all plain sail, and the cause is not hers. The measuring is in `docs/design/Report-Package90-Diana.md`, sections 12 to 17, on the branch `claude/pkg-90-diana` (read it with `git fetch origin claude/pkg-90-diana` and `git show origin/claude/pkg-90-diana:docs/design/Report-Package90-Diana.md`; her ship file and her generator are on that branch too and you may use them as a probe in a scratch copy; **nothing of hers is committed by you**).

1. **The haul.** `freesail/evolutions/scripts.py`, `TackScript.tick` (about line 2439): "mainsail haul" is called when `abs(rel) <= POINT or self.through`, her head within a point of the wind, for every ship. The script cites Luce 1884, p. 417: the after yards hauled "with the wind ahead or a point on the bow", and takes the point. A ship that comes round slowly is turned back by her after yards so hauled: the *Diana* in 20 knots comes to the haul at 1.9 knots swinging 0.7 degrees a second (the frigate of 36 at 3.3 knots and 1.2), the swung after yards give 200 to 270 kN m against her, and she falls back. With the one condition rewritten in a probe to haul with the wind ahead, she stays in 13 of 15 tacks (three headings, 8 to 25 knots) where she stays in 7; the frigate stays in 15 of 15 either way, slower in 25 knots.
2. **The fleet's count under plain sail** (three headings, 8, 12, 15, 20, 25 knots): the frigate of 36 15, the razee 13, the 74 12, the 64 13, the brig 15, the hermaphrodite brig 15; **the merchant brig 0 and the bilander 0: neither completes a tack within 900 s, in any wind, plain or shortened.** The ships of the line miss in 8 knots, which may well be true of them.
3. **"Tacked" said of a ship that swung back:** the brig's tack in 20 knots from 292.5 came through the wind, swung on round to her old tack, and was logged "Tacked; braced up on the starboard tack, heading SW by S (211°)" at 459 s. See the comments about line 2931 to 2950 of `scripts.py` for the history of that line.
4. **A jib taken in where she cannot be held without it.** `freesail/orders/storm.py`, `HEAD_SAIL_IN_RATIO = 0.75`: `shorten sail` takes every jib in at three quarters of its rating, for every ship. The *Diana*, shortened so in 25 knots, gripes to 21 degrees of helm with her rudder at its stop, comes up to 42 degrees off the wind and lies there at a knot or two; `set the jib` again brings her head off, the jib at 0.68 to 0.78 of its rating and holding 45 minutes. The frigate under the game's own captain does the same in kind when he shortens past 30 knots (gripes to 26 degrees off at 1.15 knots, taken aback twice).
5. **A light ship not shortened in time by the game's own captain.** The King's ship's doctrine (`data/captains/kings-ship.yaml`, `shorten_sail_kn` 30, shared by five ships) and the glass's rule begin the shortening at about 29 knots in a wind that took an hour to rise; the *Diana*'s watch takes the sails in turn, fourteen minutes of it, and with the wind abeam her main and mizen topgallant yards carry away at 31 knots before the hands reach them. The frigate keeps hers.
