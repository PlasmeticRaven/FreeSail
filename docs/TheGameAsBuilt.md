# The game as built

One page on what FreeSail is today, written at the close of milestone 7 (the owner's gate of 2026-11-24, `docs/gates/gate-m7.md`). It describes the build `m7`; the last section says what the trunk has gained since. The records of how each part came to be are history and stay where they are (`docs/dev/`); what milestone 7 still owes is `docs/dev/CarriedForward-M7.md`; where the game is meant to go is `docs/DesignProposal.md`.

## What you can do

Start it with `py -m freesail` and the **harbour** opens in the browser: *begin* (a first passage and four lessons), *game* (every scenario, by shelf), the *scenario maker*, *stations* and *settings*. A game is a ship, a sea and a clock you hold or let run, compressed as you like. You command by typing orders as a captain would give them, from one line hauled to a whole evolution ("tack ship", "heave to", "clear for action"), or by **standing orders** in a small language of their own that the ship follows while you are below. **The ship's log is the game:** everything that happens is a line of it, and the chart, the ship's picture and a saved game are all drawn from the same record. A game saves by name and is taken up again from the harbour; it replays exactly.

## What is simulated

- **The ship,** as her own parts: every spar, sail, line, gun and fitting, each with its load and its condition, worked by **her people one by one**, in watches, with their stations. Sail carried too long strains, warns and carries away; damage is repaired by named work.
- **Fourteen vessels and five boats:** the cutter, the smack, the lugger, the bilander, the brig, the merchant brig, the hermaphrodite brig, the topsail schooner, the privateer schooner, the corvette *Diana* (joined 27 November, after the gate), the frigate of 36, the razee of 44, the 64 and the 74. A ship may be bought, and changed in play.
- **The sea:** the Channel, Biscay, Portugal, the Strait, the Atlantic islands and Morocco, and the western Mediterranean to Sicily and Tunis; 127 ports by file, with tides, currents, pilots and prices; weather by systems, with the glass, fog and the sea breeze.
- **Navigation** by reckoning, sights and the chronometer; anchoring; boats sent ashore; **trade** by cargo bought and sold; the smack's fishing.
- **Other ships** under captains of their own by their calling (King's ships, cruisers, privateers, merchants, packets, fishermen), alone or in convoys, squadrons and a blockade; hails and **signals** in five nations' codes, drawn as flags.
- **The fight:** guns cast loose, loaded and fought, shot that tells on hull, rigging, spars and people; wounds; striking, prizes, prisoners and parole.

## Who may hold a station

A station of a ship (her captain, her master, the officer of the watch, a watcher) may be held by a person or by a **language model**, through the same orders and the same readings a player has; and a **director's** station above the game may shape the voyage within a brief. A model is asked its consent by the game before it is seated, against a written brief, and what it does is in the log under its station's name.

## How it is kept true

Period sources are cited where a figure comes from one and marked as judgement where it does not; "known truths" hold what each ship and rule must do; about nine thousand tests run in minutes and some six hundred more sail whole days, and the whole suite runs in the cloud on every tree that joins the trunk.

## Not built

The warrant officers' stations; the crew's skill, fatigue aloft and morale made true; fine control of the sails; a ship left and taken up again; a world that moves on while you are away, and a career; the data made true across 1792 to 1825. These are the proposal's milestones 8 and after.

## On the trunk since the gate's build

The flags worn by a ship's service; the harbour's start made whole; and, from the ships' package, the engine for hulls not yet in the game (a tiller's words, the whole lateen, the topsail on a pole, a boat towed astern). Of these a player of today's ships sees a tiller's words on the six vessels steered by one, and the smack's topsail handled as a topsail. The corvette *Diana*, the tartane, the polacre, the xebec and the three-decker are building.
