FreeSail, for the Mac (macOS 12 and later, Apple silicon or Intel)   download {{NAME}}
=====================================================================================

STARTING
  1. Unpack the zip: double-click it (your browser may already have done so).
     Move the folder it makes wherever you like.
  2. In that folder, Control-click (or right-click) FreeSail.command, choose
     "Open", and in the box that says "macOS cannot verify the developer of
     "FreeSail.command". Are you sure you want to open it?", click "Open".
       On macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later the box says instead "FreeSail.command"
       Not Opened: click "Done", open System Settings, Privacy & Security,
       scroll down to ""FreeSail.command" was blocked to protect your Mac",
       click "Open Anyway", give your password, and "Open Anyway" again.
     This is asked the first time only. If the Mac asks whether Terminal may
     access files in your Downloads (or Desktop, or Documents) folder, click OK.
  3. A Terminal window opens with the game's server in it, and in a few seconds
     your web browser opens the harbour. Later, double-clicking FreeSail.command
     is enough.

WHAT YOU WILL SEE
  The Terminal window: the game's server, with a few lines saying it has
  started. Keep it open while you play. The harbour is at
  http://localhost:8000/harbour (open that address yourself if the browser
  does not). Begin with "A first passage": it starts the game and its lesson.

STOPPING
  Press Control-C in the Terminal window, or close it. Save first with the
  order "save NAME" if you want to come back to the same moment.

YOUR SAVES AND SETTINGS
  They are kept outside this folder, in your Library:
  ~/Library/Application Support/FreeSail (in the Finder: Go, Go to Folder...,
  and paste that), the saves in its "saves" folder, the settings in
  settings.yaml. So for a newer download, unpack it, and move this folder to
  the Bin (the Trash): your saves and settings stay where they are.

A MODEL AT A STATION
  Optional, and needs a key of your own; see the README (game/README.md).

  Tell the person who sent you this download what you did and what the
  Terminal window said (a screenshot will do: Shift-Command-4); or, with a
